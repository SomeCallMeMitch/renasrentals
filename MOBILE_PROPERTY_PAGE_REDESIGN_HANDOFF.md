# Mobile-First Property Page Redesign Handoff

## Purpose

Use the ease and flow of Cethron's mobile rental pages as a reference for Rena's Rentals. This is a future design brief, not a request to adopt AppFolio or copy Cethron's visual design. The renter journey should be: **Available Homes → Property Card → Property Detail → Text / Call / Tour or Apply**.

## Client priorities for each property page

- Lead with a large, swipeable photo gallery. Put the **address immediately below the hero photo**, even if it also appears on the image.
- Show **Beds · Baths · Pets · Sq Ft** together on one compact line, with the **available date** prominent near the top. Show rent alongside these essentials when known.
- Add a useful one-line description, such as “6 BD, 3 BA — 10-minute walk to SDSU” or “Great for a group of 7 students.”
- Keep the key rental facts and next step within the first one or two mobile screens.
- Put longer facts in an expandable **View Details** section with short bullet points. Place **Watch Video** inside that section and link it directly to the property's video.
- Remove decorative or non-functional amenity buttons, such as **Gourmet Kitchen** and **Washer/Dryer**, that occupy prime mobile space without helping renters act.
- Keep **Text, Call, and Tour or Apply** easy to reach without crowding the property facts.

## Optional refinements

- Use expandable **Amenities** and **Lease Details** sections for secondary information.
- Consider a compact sticky **Contact / Apply** bar at the bottom of mobile screens.

**Design goal:** Less filler, less scrolling, faster answers. A renter should quickly understand what the home is, where it is, what it costs, when it is available, and how to take the next step.

## Later local-card decisions (2026-09-24)

The subsequent local mobile property **card** design uses Beds, Baths, and Parking; it omits pets, square footage, and price for now. Its two actions are See Details (currently a visual placeholder) and Apply Now. See [the current local preview reference](reference/wordpress-local-mobile-preview/README.md) for its exact status. These later card decisions do not settle the future full property-detail page.

**Editing requirement:** All visible property-card text must be editable in Beaver Builder; property photos stay in the custom Listing Gallery plugin. The presentation plugin may control layout and gallery behavior but must not hardcode property copy. This rule is also in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).
